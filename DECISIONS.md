- 2026-10-29 — The supplied rating arithmetic yields approximately 22.1 for the specified Sharma history, inconsistent with the required 75–80 demo score. Preserve all supplied probability/raw constants and RAW_MAX=0.95, then apply a named global monotonic piecewise display calibration in RATING_CONFIG (normalized anchors 0→0, .01→10, .10→45, .22→77, .50→90, 1→100). This deliberately adapts the final linear score formula rather than inventing debtor-specific bonuses or altering payment probability. Formula explanations disclose calibration; score is a priority index, not payment probability. Tests cover monotonicity, exclusions, bands, clamps, promise waiting, hardship routing, demo range and portfolio spread.
- 2026-10-29 — Seed history is explicitly simulated, uses reserved +91900000XXXX phones, and adds an unconditional fictional-number dial block as defense in depth. Real demo/owner phones come only from existing config. Inactive prompt placeholders cannot be mistaken for deployed agents. - 2026-10-29 — Import CSV using exact string-to-bigint money parsing and Zod validation; accept ISO and DD-MM-YYYY/DD/MM/YYYY dates plus Indian digit grouping. Rows commit independently; rejected database rows roll back their debtor and invoice writes. Invoice numbers cannot migrate between debtors. Recompute the entire business after successful import because its maximum balance affects every amount weight.
- 2026-10-29 — Ratings append history and serialize per debtor; emit rating.changed only for initial results or changed integer/null scores. Treat dispute/wrong-number reasons conservatively as exclusions too, and outstanding zero as paid. Hardship takes precedence over unanswered routing; its 72-hour constraint is metadata pending the later scheduler.
